## The Databricks Cost X-Ray

1. Copy three read-only queries from the tool and run them in Databricks.
2. Download each result as a CSV.
3. Drop the CSVs into the X-Ray.

You get an instant report: your 90-day baseline, cost by product, traced versus untraced spend, failed-run cost, your top 10 jobs, scheduled work running on All-Purpose compute, and clusters that look under-used.

Every figure is labelled **Measured** or **Estimated**, and every estimate shows the rule behind it. Untraced spend is shown as a visibility gap, never as savings.

## Why your security team won't mind

The X-Ray runs entirely in your browser. Your CSVs are never uploaded anywhere, not to us and not to anyone else. Close the tab and the data is gone.

Not ready to run queries yet? Press **Try it with sample data** and you'll see a full report in one click.
