The evidence that strengthens a renewal takes time to build. Thirty days out, you can only argue about price. Six months out, you can change what you're committing to.

## What to have ready, and when

- **180 days out:** a 90-day baseline and your traced percentage.
- **120 days out:** your top 10 jobs with owners, and failed-run cost.
- **90 days out:** a full assessment, with measured and estimated figures separated.
- **60 days out:** a job-by-job classification and a keep-versus-move list.
- **30 days out:** a negotiation pack built on evidence, not the invoice.
