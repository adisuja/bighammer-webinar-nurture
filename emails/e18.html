If you made the list of your 20 most expensive jobs, here's how we turn it into decisions.

Two scores per job

We score every job on two things: how complex it is, and how dependent it is on Databricks-specific features. That gives five outcomes:

Keep on Databricks: heavily dependent (ML, DLT, streaming and similar). It's where they belong.
Migrate: simple and low-dependency. A candidate to run elsewhere.
Optimise, then evaluate: complex but low-dependency. Tune it first, decide later.
Evaluate: in between, or not enough information yet.
Defer: too small in cost to be worth moving now.

Every classification comes with its scores and the reasons behind them. No black box.

What good looks like

Decisions made job by job, never platform-wide.
ML and deeply integrated work stays where it earns its price.
Simple, low-dependency batch jobs reviewed as candidates to move.

And when a job should move

For jobs in the Migrate group, BigHammer Migrate converts Scala JAR and PySpark wheel jobs to run on GCP Dataproc, replacing Databricks-specific code such as dbutils widgets and table-name-based Delta access. The original job keeps running until the new one has proven itself, which is the subject of the final step.

The Field Guide includes a scoring worksheet if you want to try this on your own top 20 first.
