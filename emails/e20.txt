WHY "IT RAN SUCCESSFULLY" ISN'T ENOUGH

Code conversion is the visible part of a migration. Proving that the new job produces the same data as the old one is the part that gets skipped, and it's exactly where trust breaks.

A job can complete on time and still produce different results:

- A changed join quietly drops rows that used to match.
- A different null handling rule turns missing values into zeros.
- A timezone shift moves every timestamp by an hour.

None of these fail the run. All of them fail the business. And they're usually found by a finance analyst three weeks later, not by the pipeline.

> A green tick on the run tells you the job finished. It tells you nothing about whether it was right.
