Tick a step only if you could show the evidence today, not if you could produce it with a week's notice:

1. See it: you have a 90-day baseline by product and SKU.
2. Trace it: you know what share of spend belongs to a job.
3. Measure it: you know what failed runs cost last quarter.
4. Assess it: you've run a read-only assessment of your workspace.
5. Report it: your figures are split into measured and estimated.
6. Right-size it: your largest clusters are sized from real CPU data.
7. Classify it: every expensive job has a keep, optimise or move decision.
8. Prove it: you have a parity process for anything that moves.
