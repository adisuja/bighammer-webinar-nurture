Last time I said the detail behind your invoice is already in your workspace. Today, here's how to get it out.

Three system tables do almost all of the work:


- system.billing.usage tells you what was consumed, when, on which SKU, and (crucially) which job and job run it belonged to, in usage_metadata.
- system.billing.list_prices tells you what that SKU cost at the time.
- system.lakeflow.job_run_timeline tells you how each run ended.

THE DETAIL THAT TRIPS PEOPLE UP

Prices change over time, so the join to list_prices has to respect the window in which each price was valid. Join on SKU alone and you'll double-count rows whenever a price changed during your 90 days. Here's the core of it:

SELECT u.usage_date, u.sku_name,
       u.usage_metadata.job_id,
       u.usage_quantity * p.pricing.default AS list_cost
FROM system.billing.usage u
JOIN system.billing.list_prices p
  ON  u.sku_name   = p.sku_name
  AND u.cloud      = p.cloud
  AND u.usage_unit = p.usage_unit
  AND u.usage_start_time >= p.price_start_time
  AND (p.price_end_time IS NULL OR u.usage_start_time < p.price_end_time)
WHERE u.usage_date >= current_date() - INTERVAL 90 DAYS

Group that by job_id and you have your top jobs by cost. Group by sku_name and you have the invoice, explained.
