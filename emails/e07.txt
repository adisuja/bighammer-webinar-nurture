Last week we looked at why so much Databricks spend has no job attached. The good news: most of the fix is configuration, not migration.


FIX 1: SCHEDULED PRODUCTION WORK RUNS ON JOBS COMPUTE

A notebook scheduled on an All-Purpose cluster does the same work as a job on a job cluster. Two things change when you move it:

- Every run becomes traceable. Job clusters write the job and run IDs into billing usage, so the spend has a name on it.
- It's billed at a lower rate. Jobs compute is priced below All-Purpose compute on Databricks' published list prices. The exact gap depends on your cloud and tier, so check your own rate card, but it's rarely small.

FIX 2: A SMALL REQUIRED TAG SET, ENFORCED BY CLUSTER POLICY

Three tags are enough to start: owner, cost_centre and env. Make them required in a cluster policy so a cluster can't be created without them. Tags flow into custom_tags on billing usage, which means showback by team stops being a spreadsheet exercise.

WHAT GOOD LOOKS LIKE

- Scheduled production work on Jobs compute, so every run is traceable.
- Three required tags, enforced by policy, not by memo.
- A coverage target, such as "most spend traceable to a job", reviewed monthly.

> Nobody did anything wrong. The guardrails just weren't there yet.
